## Cautionary Tales

### What do AQR and PIMCO know that we don't?

- AQR
  - Tale:
    - launched AQR Re in this space around 2011
      - hired a bunch of industry veterans
      - invested a ton into spinning up operations
      - posted decent returns...
    - yet, shuttered the unit within 5 years (around 2015)
      - without a clear impetus
      - citing only a corporate-safe excuse
        about operational complexity and capacity.
- Allianz/PIMCO
  - Tale:
    - launched their own ILS platform around 2019
      - *also* hired a bunch of industry veterans
      - *also* invested a ton into spinning up operations
      - *also* posted decent returns...
    - and yet, *also* shuttered their unit within 5 years (around 2023)
      - *also* without a clear impetus
      - *also* citing only a corporate-safe excuse
        about operational complexity and capacity.
  - Notes:
    - The PIMCO ILS team went on to found King Ridge Capital,
      subadviser on the `TXSE:ILS` ETF from above.
    - Around 2024, Allianz/PIMCO relaunched
      two separate fresh reinsurance ventures.
      - Pando Re, where the cedant/sponsor (original insurer) is
        Aspen Insurance's casualty/liability business
        (so, in particular, *not* dealing with catastrophe/disaster).
      - Sconset Re, where the cedant/sponsor (original insurer) is
        Allianz Insurance's own life/annuity business
        (so, again, *not* catastrophe/disaster).
